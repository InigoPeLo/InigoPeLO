### 🧠 About Me

I'm a **Master's student in Theoretical Physics** at Universidad Complutense de Madrid, specializing in **quantum computing**. My work sits at the intersection of statistical mechanics, quantum algorithms, and classical computation.

- 🔭 Currently building a **hybrid quantum-classical shortest-path solver** — combining Dürr–Høyer/BBHT quantum minimum search with Dijkstra's algorithm
- ⚛️ Background in **Monte Carlo simulation** of the 3D Ising model & SU(2) lattice gauge theory
- 🧩 Comfortable moving between **Cirq/qsim** and **PennyLane** for quantum simulation
- 📈 Actively transitioning into **data science**, using quantum computing as a differentiator
- 📍 Based in Madrid, Spain
- 💬 Ask me about finite-size scaling, oracle-based quantum search, or why Erdős–Rényi graphs matter for benchmarking
